<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Oman Air splits its baggage rules into two parts: cabin (hand) baggage and checked baggage.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cabin baggage stays fairly consistent across fare types, but checked baggage swings widely depending on whether you book Super Saver, Comfort, or Flex.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The airline also runs a weight-based system for checked bags in most markets, meaning your total baggage weight matters more than the number of pieces, as long as no single bag goes over the maximum.</span></p>

<h2><strong>Cabin (Hand) Baggage Allowance</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cabin baggage rules apply the same way across almost every fare type:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Economy Class:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> One cabin bag up to 7kg, plus one personal item such as a laptop bag or small handbag</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Business Class and Business Studio:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Two cabin bags, each up to 7kg, plus one personal item</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Size limit:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Total dimensions (length + width + height) must not exceed 115cm for cabin bags</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This 7kg hand baggage limit applies on every Oman Air flight, even the cheapest Economy Super Saver fare. That's more generous than several competing carriers, which sometimes restrict the lowest fare tier to a personal item only.</span></p>

<h3><strong>The April 2026 Enhanced Routes Update</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Oman Air made a notable change to its baggage policy in April 2026, boosting Economy checked baggage by up to 20kg on select routes.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The enhanced routes are the Indian Subcontinent, Cairo, Manila, Dar es Salaam, Baghdad, and Zanzibar.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">On these routes, even the Super Saver fare now includes a 20kg checked bag, something that wasn't available before on the lowest fare tier.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If your trip touches one of these destinations, it's worth checking your booking confirmation carefully.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You might already have checked baggage included on a fare that traditionally offered none.</span></p>
<h2><strong>Business Class Baggage: What You Actually Get</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Business Class and Business Studio passengers get noticeably more room to pack.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beyond the two cabin bags allowed onboard, checked baggage typically runs between 40kg and 60kg depending on the specific Business fare booked.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Business bags also receive priority tagging, so they tend to come off the carousel faster on arrival.</span></p>
<h2><strong>Traveling with Infants</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Passengers travelling with an infant under two years old get an extra 10kg of checked baggage allowance.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This applies across all fare types with one exception: the Economy Super Saver fare does not include the infant allowance, so families booking the cheapest ticket should factor this in before they pack.</span></p>
<h2><strong>Sindbad Loyalty Members Get Extra Weight</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Oman Air's frequent flyer program, Sindbad, adds baggage weight on top of your fare allowance depending on your membership tier:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Silver members:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> An additional 10kg</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Gold members:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> An additional 20kg</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you fly Oman Air regularly, building up Sindbad status is one of the simplest ways to stop worrying about excess baggage fees altogether.</span></p>
<h2><strong>Sports Equipment, Musical Instruments, and Special Items</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Oversized items like sports equipment or musical instruments are generally accepted as checked baggage but may count toward your standard allowance or require an additional fee, depending on size and weight.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It's best to contact Oman Air or check the fare brand details before your flight if you're travelling with items like golf bags, ski equipment, or a musical instrument that won't fit in a standard case.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pets are accepted in the cargo hold rather than the cabin. If your pet and its carrier together weigh more than 75kg or the combined dimensions exceed 292cm, the airline typically requires shipping as cargo instead of standard checked baggage.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fees vary by destination and carrier size, so this is worth confirming directly with Oman Air well ahead of travel.</span></p>

<h2><strong>Prohibited and Restricted Items</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Standard international rules apply for both cabin and checked baggage.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In the cabin, avoid packing sharp objects with a blade or point over 6cm, tools like screwdrivers or wrenches, flammable liquids and aerosols, and most lighters or matchboxes.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Power banks must always travel in the cabin and are never permitted in checked baggage. Liquids in carry-on bags need to follow the standard 100ml container rule.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For the latest international baggage safety guidance,</span><a href="https://www.iata.org/bags/index"> <span style="font-weight: 400;">IATA's passenger baggage rules</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> explain how airlines handle restricted items, lithium batteries, power banks, liquids, and other items in carry-on and checked baggage.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Passengers departing from India should also note that the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) enforces a separate one-cabin-bag rule that can override Oman Air's standard two-piece Business Class cabin allowance on those specific departures.</span></p>
<h2><strong>Frequently Asked Questions</strong></h2>
<h3><strong>Does Oman Air charge for the first checked bag?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It depends on your fare. Comfort and Flex fares in both Economy and Business include checked baggage. Economy Super Saver does not include a checked bag, except on the enhanced routes added in April 2026.</span></p>
<h3><strong>How much does Oman Air charge per kilo for excess baggage?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Rates vary by route, but prepaying online through Oman Air's Extra Bags option is consistently cheaper than paying the per-kilo rate at the airport counter.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Can I combine two checked bags to meet my weight allowance?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Yes. Oman Air generally uses a total-weight system, so you can split your allowance across multiple bags as long as no single piece exceeds 32kg and your combined weight stays within your fare's limit.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Does Sindbad status increase my baggage allowance?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Yes. Silver members receive an extra 10kg, and Gold members receive an extra 20kg on top of their fare's standard allowance.</span></p>
